## Database lineage (MAJ-2)

`0017_player_defense_events` adds `blocked` / `dodges` / `interrupts` to
`fight_player_summaries`. Local main's `0022_player_summary_defense_actions`
re-added `dodges` / `interrupts` to the same table, which would be a no-op at
best.

**The first version of this record was wrong, and verifying it on a real
PostgreSQL is what exposed the real story.** The columns 0017 adds do not reach
`0022` at all: `8b674a6a9cfc_phase3_schema_changes` — an autogenerated migration
whose model snapshot lagged the live schema — **drops** `blocked`, `dodges` and
`interrupts`, along with `damage_taken`, `deaths`, `downs`, `stun_breaks`,
`fights.context` and `fight_agents.position_samples`. So the revision `0022`
revises (`ea3023c87c0f`) has none of them, and `0022`'s `blocked -> blocks`
rename branch is unreachable on the linear chain.

Resolution: keep `0017` untouched; `0022` re-adds the trio under the product's
names, is idempotent, and its `downgrade()` drops exactly what its `upgrade()`
is responsible for. The later fix matters: the first version renamed `blocks`
back to `blocked` and left the two other columns behind, so
`alembic downgrade -1` did not restore the target revision even though the CI
round-trip passed on exit codes alone.

Verified end to end on PostgreSQL 16.15 — fresh upgrade, upgrade from the
representative prior revision, pre-existing row preserved, downgrade -1,
re-upgrade. Evidence and the two defects: `docs/validation/migration-round-trip.md`.
Alembic reports a single head (`0022`).

**The consumer audit that followed this record corrected its consequence, not
its cause.** An earlier revision claimed the two columns still advertised by
`apps/api/src/gw2analytics_api/schemas/fight.py` (`damage_taken`, `stun_breaks`)
have "nothing populating them". They are populated on every request: the Combat
Readout derives them from the event blob (`PlayerDefenseAggregator`,
`PlayerHealAggregator`), and the ORM never needed a mapping to do it. Persistence
loss and runtime/API feature loss are different things, and this migration
caused the first, not the second.

The defect stands as described above — the migration dropped nine columns that
four deliberate migrations had added — but no stored data was lost either, since
the application never wrote any of them (traced in
`docs/validation/phase3-removed-column-consumer-audit.md`). The audit also
settles the remedy: no forward migration is needed. `0022` already re-adds the
three counters with a durable consumer; the rest are derived on read or were
never wired to anything.

A duplicated select-label defect inherited from the source branch was also
fixed in the per-account aggregate query.

## Regeneration / buff_state (three-way)

Three mutually inconsistent, mutually unverified proposals existed:

1. dirty owner-root `buff_state.py` (regeneration queue extension rework);
2. dirty uptime-worktree `buff_state.py` (QueueLogic.Activate semantics);
3. the committed certified lineage (`reconcile/uptime-semantics`,
   `rework/wave6-uptime-f7e73e5`).

Decision: **preserve (3) as semantic/certification knowledge; do NOT integrate
the code.** An earlier revision of this record said "adopt (3)", which read as if
those commits had been merged into the candidate. They have not been, and they
should not be: both branches rewrite `libs/gw2_evtc_parser/.../parser.py`,
`statechange_dispatch.py` and the analytics that consume its event stream — the
parts the refoundation exists to delete — and the executed EI field-coverage
gate has since shown the raw buff event stream is `MISSING` from the EI export,
so uptime semantics must be restated against whatever replaces the parser.

All three proposals (1), (2) and (3) are preserved in the recovery root. The
durable, parser-agnostic acceptance baseline is
`scripts/ei-parity/corpus-baseline.json` (per-path EI-vs-product deltas over the
35-log corpus). The eleven semantic expectations the branches encode, and the
ownership-statechange-constant contradiction they expose against the shipped
code, are recorded in `docs/validation/regeneration-decision.md`.
